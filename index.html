Our Story

A bakery built on butter, patience, and two traditions.

East & West began as a simple idea: dessert shouldn't have to choose a side. Our kitchen runs two counters under one roof — a French patisserie bench on one, a copper qatayef pan on the other — both fed by the same obsession with doing it properly.
